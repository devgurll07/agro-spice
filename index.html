About Agro Spice & Seasonings Pvt. Ltd.

Rooted in Rajasthan’s Spice Heartland, Built for International Food Supply Chains.

Operating from the dedicated Food Park at Matsya Industrial Area (MIA), Alwar, Rajasthan, Agro Spice & Seasonings Pvt. Ltd. bridges authentic Indian spice sourcing with precision industrial seasoning formulation.

Whether you are an extruded snack brand seeking an addictive Masala Munch profile, a fryums manufacturer optimizing seasoning adhesion, or an overseas food importer sourcing bulk Indian spices, we deliver batch-to-batch consistency, microbial safety, and export-compliant packaging.
